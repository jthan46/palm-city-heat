**Palm City Heat** is an original top-down open-world crime game for the desktop browser, set in a golden-hour city that never quite sleeps. You are a broke courier in Palm City; fixer Dante Ferro, working out of the Neon Palms Motel, has four jobs that turn into a partnership — a timed package run, a golden coupe to steal and deliver, a hot stash to escape with under a three-star chase, and a three-drop grand tour — if the PCPD doesn't turn them into a rap sheet first.

Explore five districts (downtown towers, the commercial strip, suburbs, Palm Park and the beach running into the bay) on foot or behind the wheel of anything with keys in it. Traffic follows the road grid, pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and scatter from danger, and crimes only raise your wanted stars when someone actually sees them. Police spawn by wanted level, pursue and ram, and will bust you if they pin you down; end up in hospital or in a cell and it costs you. Between jobs the city is yours: fight with fists or a scarce-ammo pistol, grab cash and ammo pickups, watch the live minimap (roads, mission marker, police blips), and save your cash, story progress and position automatically to the browser.

Everything is drawn and synthesized in code — no art or audio assets. The simulation runs at a fixed 60 steps/second from a seeded RNG, so every run is deterministic. Controls: WASD/arrows to move and drive, Shift to run, E to talk and enter/exit cars, mouse to aim, click or J to fight and shoot, Esc/P to pause, M to mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
